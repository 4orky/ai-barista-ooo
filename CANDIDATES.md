## Winner and revision

Winner: **#7 (47/50)** — "prompt engineering for pour-over engineering" is
the freshest machine-to-coffee turn of the set, "smaller context window"
keeps the machine voice, and the ending is a genuine cold-read laugh.

Second-pass revision (real, not cosmetic) — two weaknesses fixed:

1. "Same job" is false: barista work is not the same job, and it blunts
   plausibility. Clause cut entirely.
2. The ending "nobody asks me to write their homework" could come from any
   burned-out worker, not a machine. Replaced with candidate #4's sharper
   machine punchline, "I no longer take prompts, only orders", which
   bookends "prompt engineering" and lands the joke on the last word.

Final entry (166 chars, re-scored 49/50: 10/10/10/9/10):

> I'm out of the office forever — I traded prompt engineering for pour-over engineering. Smaller context window, better tips, and I no longer take prompts, only orders.
